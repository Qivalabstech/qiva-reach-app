# C1 · Idea generator

**Status:** Approved 14 Oct 2026. Built; PostHog events, help note, Arman's relevance rating (`docs/quality/c1-relevance-sheet.md`) and a Safari check still to do before "Accepted".

Build notes: the model is asked for 25 ideas and 20 are shown; the top-up asks for the missing number plus 5. The daily guard counts model calls (60, about 30 clicks). The prompt sends at most 60 recently shown ideas, while duplicates are filtered against up to 200 from the last 24 hours. Known limit: the model sometimes puts a made-up detail in an idea ("from five tiers to two"); ideas are only angles, and the writer (C2) uses only facts the user gives.

## User story
As a user who does not know what to post, I click one button and get 20 post ideas for my niche, so I never start from a blank page.

## Acceptance criteria (locked)
20 niche-relevant ideas per click in under 10 seconds; no duplicates within a session.

## Screens
1. **/app/ideas** "Post ideas"
   - Short line: "Click for 20 ideas based on your goal and niche."
   - Optional field "Focus on" (for example "lessons from client work", max 100 characters).
   - Button "Get 20 ideas" (the one Marigold action). Each click replaces the list with 20 fresh ideas.
   - Each idea is one plain line (max 120 characters) with a button "Write this post", which opens C2 with the idea filled in as the topic.
   - Missing goal or niche: "Tell us your goal and niche first." with a link to Settings.
   - Failure: "We couldn't get ideas. Try again." The previous list stays on screen.
2. A link "Post ideas" on the /app home.

## How the 20 ideas are made
- One fast-model call (`ANTHROPIC_MODEL_FAST`) with: the user's goal, niche, optional focus, the first lines of their last 5 imported posts (so ideas do not repeat what they already wrote) and the list of ideas already shown in this session.
- Asks for a mix of formats: story, lesson, opinion, how-to, question, list, myth to bust.
- Ideas are topics and angles only. No hashtags, no made-up statistics, no banned phrases (the voice profile's list applies).
- "Session" means the last 24 hours. The server drops any idea that matches one already shown to this workspace in that window (case and spacing ignored). If fewer than 20 remain, it asks once more to top up. If it still cannot reach 20, it shows what it has and says so.

## Data
- Table `ideas` already exists (`workspace_id`, `text`, `created_at`). Every shown idea is stored; this is also the memory used to avoid duplicates. The `saved` column is not used here (saving ideas is the swipe file, I2, in Phase 5).
- Logged in `ai_usage` (feature `idea_generation`). Prompt: `src/ai/prompts/ideas.v1.ts`.
- Limit: 30 clicks a day per user, with a clear message. Plan limits come with billing in Phase 2.
- Row-level security: members of the workspace only.

## Compliance and privacy
User's own data only. Nothing is read from LinkedIn. The first lines of the user's imported posts go to the AI provider, which the privacy policy covers (Q-004).

## Behaviour rules
- Time: under 10 seconds from click to 20 ideas on screen.
- PostHog events: `ideas_generated` (count), `idea_opened_in_writer`.

## Acceptance tests
1. Unit: duplicate filter ignores case, spacing and punctuation; top-up logic stops after one extra call.
2. Stubbed model: one click shows exactly 20 ideas, none empty, none over 120 characters.
3. Stubbed model that repeats earlier ideas on purpose: the second click still shows 20 with no repeats from the first.
4. Real model on the live site, 5 clicks in a row: every click returns 20, no duplicates across the 5 lists, time under 10 seconds each, logged in `ai_usage`.
5. Banned phrases never appear in a shown idea.
6. Relevance check by a person: 3 niches, 20 ideas each; Arman rates each idea relevant or not; at least 80% relevant.
7. "Write this post" opens C2 with the idea as the topic.
8. Another user cannot read these ideas (RLS). 390 px phone, keyboard only, visible focus.
