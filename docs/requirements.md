# Qiva Reach — Requirements

Source of truth for scope: `company-brain/01-plan-and-scope.md` (Scope v1.0, locked). This file summarises the part being built now.

## What We're Building
Qiva Reach is an AI LinkedIn post writer, scheduler and engagement community. Phase 1 (26 Oct–8 Nov 2026, started early under D-008) delivers the foundation: a new user signs up, teaches the app their writing voice, and gets a LinkedIn post draft that sounds like them in under 5 minutes. Nothing is ever automated on the user's LinkedIn account.

## Target Users
- Primary: English-speaking founders, consultants, creators and B2B marketers who post weekly on LinkedIn (1,000–20,000 followers).
- Secondary: agency owners (Phase 5), job seekers.

## Core Features (Phase 1)
A1 Sign in with LinkedIn · A2 Email and Google sign-up · A3 Goal and niche setup · A4 Voice import · B1 Voice profile · B2 Tone presets · C1 Idea generator · C2 Post writer · C3 Hook lab. Acceptance criteria: `company-brain/01-plan-and-scope.md`.

## Out of Scope
Everything from Phase 2 onward: scheduling, Circles, Chrome extension, billing, admin. Never built: auto-likes/comments/follows, scraping, stored LinkedIn passwords or cookies.

## Success Criteria (Gate G1)
All 9 features accepted · 10 test users reach a draft in under 5 minutes · 7 of 10 rate voice match 4–5 out of 5 · row-level security verified on every table.
