Story and positioning

The brand idea comes from the most important line on LinkedIn: the dashed fold where a post says "see more". Everything we make helps people earn the click past that line.

Mission

Help every professional grow on LinkedIn in their own voice, without hiring a ghostwriter or risking their account.

Promise

You approve every word. We do the drafting, the scheduling, the reminding and the measuring.

Personality

A sharp, warm friend who has read a thousand posts: direct, encouraging, never salesy, never robotic.

Qiva Reach is the LinkedIn growth co-pilot that sounds like you and never risks your account.

Logo

The ring is your circle. The tail turns it into a Q. The marigold dot sits just outside the ring: the reach beyond your current network. The dot stays marigold on every background except marigold itself, where the whole logo goes Indigo Ink.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the dot's diameter on every side. Minimum width: 96 px for the lockup, 20 px for the mark.

Don't

The mark is simple, so small changes break it quickly.

Colour

Indigo Ink and Porcelain do most of the work. Marigold is a highlighter: use it to mark the one thing that matters on a screen, never as a background for long text. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Voice and tone

We talk like a smart friend who happens to know LinkedIn well. Short sentences. Plain words. We sell outcomes (consistency, reach, leads), never automation.

Direct

Say the thing in the first line. No warm-up.

Warm

Encourage people who are nervous about posting.

Honest

No fake numbers, no "guaranteed viral", no hype.

Local

Rupees, IST, Indian examples. Hinglish is fine in social, not in product UI.

Imagery and icons

Show real work: drafts, calendars, comment threads, people at desks in Indian cities. No stock handshakes, no glowing robot brains, no fake screenshots of LinkedIn's own interface.

Icons

Outline, 1.8 px stroke, rounded caps, 24 px grid. Use one icon set (Lucide matches this style).

Photography

Natural light, real workspaces, founders and consultants mid-task. Crop tight. Apply no filters except a slight warm white balance. Never place text over faces.

The fold motif

The dashed marigold line is our signature graphic. Use it once per layout: under a hook, between a teaser and the full story, or as a section divider.

Phase plan

        
Seven phases from 12 Oct 2026 to 30 Jun 2027. A phase starts only when the previous exit gate is fully passed. Select a phase to see its work packages, gate, budget and features. Ticks are saved in this browser.

      

      

      


      

        

          
Scope v1.0 is locked

          
    
            
  1. No feature is added to any phase.
    2. 
            
  2. No feature is removed, split or cut down.
    3. 
            
  3. No feature moves between phases.
    4. 
            
  4. No acceptance criterion is weakened.
    5. 
            
  5. New ideas go to the parking lot, reviewed after 30 Jun 2027.

The only exceptions

          
    
            
  1. Compliance: LinkedIn, Chrome Web Store, payment, GST or data-protection rules require it.
    2. 
            
  2. Security: it fixes a vulnerability or data-exposure risk.

Each needs written approval from both Designated Partners. If a phase runs late, the date moves; the scope does not.
