# C3 · Hook lab

**Status:** Approved 14 Oct 2026. Built; PostHog events, help note, Arman's favourite-hook check and a Safari check still to do before "Accepted".

Build notes: hooks over 120 characters are dropped (that is the definition of a hook); hooks over 100 characters are capped at score 6. The model is asked for 12 hooks and the top 10 are shown; if fewer than 10 survive the rules it asks once for 8 more, and shows what it has if at least 5 are valid. The daily guard counts model calls (80, about 40 lists). The fast model runs with its hidden "thinking" turned off for this call, which makes it faster (about 4 to 5 seconds) and stops thinking tokens from using up the answer limit.

## User story
As a user with a draft, I see 10 different opening lines for it, each scored, and I swap one in with a single click, because the first line decides whether anyone clicks "see more".

## Acceptance criteria (locked)
10 hook options per post, each with a 1–10 strength score and one-click swap.

## What a hook is
The first line of the post (everything before the first line break), at most 120 characters. The rest of the post is never changed by this tool.

## Screens
1. **Inside /app/write/[id]**, under the draft: button "Improve the hook" (Ink, not Marigold; the screen's one Marigold action is "Write my post" from C2).
2. **Hook panel** opens below the button:
   - "Your current first line" at the top with its own score.
   - 10 options below, best score first. Each shows: the hook text, a type label (question, bold claim, number, story, contrarian, curiosity, direct promise, short punch), the score as "7 / 10" and one short reason ("Names a concrete result").
   - A button "Use this hook" on each. One click replaces the first line of the draft, saves, and marks that option "In your post". The old first line stays in the list as "Your current first line", so the user can swap back with one click.
   - Small note: "Scores are our estimate of how strong an opening is. They don't predict views."
   - Button "Show 10 more" regenerates the list. Failure: "We couldn't get hooks. Try again."

## How scores are made
- One fast-model call (`ANTHROPIC_MODEL_FAST`) with the full draft, the voice profile and the preset. It writes 10 hooks of different types and rates each hook and the current first line from 1 to 10 against a written rubric: specific over vague, a reason to keep reading, true to what the post delivers, short and clear, sounds like the user, no cliché.
- The server then applies fixed rules: over 100 characters caps the score at 6, and over 120 characters removes the hook; a generic opener ("I'm excited to share", "Let's talk about") caps at 4; a banned phrase removes the hook; a number that is not in the post removes the hook. Scores are whole numbers, and the list is sorted high to low.
- Hooks must be true to the post. They may not promise something the post does not contain.
- Prompt: `src/ai/prompts/hook-lab.v1.ts`. Logged in `ai_usage` (feature `hook_lab`).

## Data
- `posts.hook` stores the current first line; `posts.body` is updated with the swap. No migration needed.
- Limit: 40 hook lists a day per user (guard only).
- Row-level security: owners and editors write; members read.

## Behaviour rules
- Time: under 10 seconds.
- Keyboard: the list works with Tab and Enter; scores are readable by a screen reader ("Hook 3 of 10, score 7 out of 10").
- PostHog events: `hooks_generated`, `hook_swapped` (type and score, no text).

## Acceptance tests
1. Unit: first-line split (with and without a blank line, one-line posts), the swap leaves every character after the first line unchanged, swap and swap-back restore the exact original.
2. Unit: rules cap long and generic hooks, drop banned and invented-number hooks, and sort by score.
3. Stubbed model: exactly 10 options plus the current line; every score a whole number 1–10; sorted.
4. Stubbed model returning 12 hooks, 3 of them over 120 characters or with banned phrases: the panel still shows 10 valid ones (asks again once to top up).
5. One-click swap changes only line one; "Your current first line" can be swapped back.
6. Real model on the live site, 10 posts: 10 valid hooks each time, at least 3 different scores in each list, under 10 seconds, logged.
7. Human check: for 10 posts, Arman picks his favourite hook; it is in our top 3 for at least 6 of 10.
8. Another user cannot read or change these hooks (RLS). 390 px phone, keyboard only, visible focus.
