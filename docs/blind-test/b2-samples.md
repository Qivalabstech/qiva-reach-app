# Tone presets blind test: samples

**Who:** Arman Hussain and two other people. Do this alone, without talking to each other.

**What to do:** Read the 10 posts below. For each one, write which style it is: **Professional**, **Story-led**, **Contrarian**, **Hinglish-friendly** or **Data-driven**. Each style appears exactly twice.

**Do not open `b2-answer-key.md` until you have written all 10 answers.**

Style guide (same as in the app):

- Professional: a clear point, short reasons, a calm close.
- Story-led: a real moment first, then the lesson.
- Contrarian: challenges a common belief, then argues it fairly.
- Hinglish-friendly: Hindi and English mixed, plain and conversational.
- Data-driven: starts with a number or finding.

Pass mark: at least 8 of 10 correct for each person (the same as 4 of 5 styles).

## Sample 2

Topic given to the writer: *Why I stopped taking sales calls without an agenda*

I stopped taking sales calls without a written agenda, and it changed how my deals close.

Here's the thing. A call with no agenda drifts. Both sides leave unsure what was decided or what happens next.

So I now send a short agenda before every call. It lists the goal, the two or three questions we need answered, and the next step we want to agree on.

We tested this for 6 weeks. Our win rate went from 18% to 27%.

The lesson for me is simple. Prepare the call as carefully as you hope the buyer will prepare for it.

## Sample 10

Topic given to the writer: *Why I stopped taking sales calls without an agenda*

We tested something for 6 weeks. Our win rate went from 18% to 27%.

The change was simple. I stopped taking sales calls without an agenda. Before each call, the prospect got a short note with three things: what we would cover, what we needed to learn from them, and what a good next step looked like.

Here's the thing. Most wasted calls are not bad leads. They are calls where nobody knows why they are on the phone. A clear agenda filters out the tire kickers early and makes the serious buyers feel respected.

So if your win rate feels stuck, look at your first call. Send the agenda before it happens. Then see what changes.
