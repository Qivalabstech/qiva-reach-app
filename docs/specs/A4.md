# A4 · Voice import

**Status:** Approved by Razam Sir 13 Oct 2026. Built; PostHog events, help note and a real LinkedIn export check still to do before "Accepted".

Build notes: the export file is read in the browser (limit 10 MB), so only post text (max 20 posts, 20,000 characters each) reaches the server, instead of a 200 KB file limit. "Over 20 posts" keeps the first 20 for pasted text and the 20 newest for an export. Tested with a hand-made Shares.csv and zip; Arman's test users' real exports are the real check.

## User story
As a new user, I paste some of my past LinkedIn posts, or upload my LinkedIn data export, so Qiva Reach can learn how I write.

## Acceptance criteria (locked)
Accepts 5–20 pasted posts or the LinkedIn data-export file; fewer than 3 posts shows a clear message.

## Screens
1. **/onboarding (step 2 of 2)** "Teach us your voice"
   - Tab 1 "Paste posts": one large box; posts separated by a line containing only `---`. Live counter: "7 posts found".
   - Tab 2 "Upload LinkedIn export": file picker for `Shares.csv` (or the zip) from LinkedIn's "Get a copy of your data". Short help: "Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Posts".
   - Under 3 posts: "We need at least 3 posts to learn your voice. Paste a few more."
   - 3–4 posts: allowed with a note "More posts (5 or more) give a closer match."
   - Over 20 posts: uses the 20 most recent and says so.
   - Button "Learn my voice" (Marigold). Then a short progress state, then B1 voice profile screen.
   - Skip option "I'll do this later" leads to the app with an empty-state: "No drafts yet. Paste a few old posts and we'll learn your voice."

## Data
- Each post saved to `posts` with `status = 'imported'` (private to the workspace, RLS).
- The upload is parsed in the browser/server in memory; the file itself is not stored.
- Posts are deduplicated and trimmed; posts under 40 characters ignored.

## Compliance
User supplies their own text and their own export. We never open LinkedIn or read LinkedIn pages. Passes the gate.

## Behaviour rules
- Max 20,000 characters per post, 200 KB per upload; clear errors otherwise.
- Importing again adds new posts and skips duplicates.
- PostHog events: `voice_import_started`, `voice_import_completed` (count only, no content).

## Acceptance tests (Playwright + Vitest)
1. Parser unit tests: separators, blank lines, duplicates, short posts, CSV with quoted newlines, 20-post cap.
2. 2 posts shows the clear message; 5 posts proceeds.
3. A real `Shares.csv` sample parses to the right number of posts.
4. Imported posts are invisible to other users (RLS check).
5. Works at 390 px; error text is announced to screen readers.
