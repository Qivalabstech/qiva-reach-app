# 05 · Brand summary (v1.0, October 2026)

Full interactive kit: `qiva-reach-brand-kit.html` (logo files, templates, tokens, email signature). This file is the quick reference every executive can rely on.

## Idea

The brand comes from the most important line on LinkedIn: the dashed fold where a post says "see more". Everything Qiva Reach makes helps people earn the click past that line.

- **Mission:** help every professional grow on LinkedIn in their own voice, without hiring a ghostwriter or risking their account.
- **Promise:** you approve every word; we do the drafting, scheduling, reminding and measuring.
- **Personality:** a sharp, warm friend who has read a thousand posts. Direct, encouraging, never salesy, never robotic.
- **Positioning line:** Qiva Reach is the LinkedIn growth co-pilot that sounds like you and never risks your account.
- **Hero line on the website:** "Write and schedule LinkedIn posts in your own voice."

## Logo

- Mark: a ring (your circle) with a tail that makes a Q, and a marigold dot outside the ring (reach beyond your network).
- Wordmark: "qiva reach", lowercase, Bricolage Grotesque, weight 750.
- The dot is always marigold, except on a marigold background, where the whole logo is Indigo Ink.
- Clear space = the dot's diameter on every side. Minimum width: 96 px for the lockup, 20 px for the mark.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour the dot, or add shadows or effects.
- App icon and favicon: white mark on an Indigo Ink rounded square.

Approved pairs: white on Ink, Ink on Porcelain, Ink on Marigold, white on Peacock. Never long text on Marigold. No LinkedIn blue.

## Typography

- **Bricolage Grotesque** (display, 600–800): headlines, buttons, numbers. Tight letter-spacing on large sizes.
- **Figtree** (body and UI, 400–700): everything people read for longer.
- Scale: Display 72, H1 48, H2 32, H3 22, Body large 20, Body 17, Small 14 (px).
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps labels.

## Shape and components

- Corners: 12 px for controls, 20 px for panels and cards.
- Primary button: Indigo Ink. Accent button (one per screen): Marigold with Ink text.
- Focus ring: 4 px Marigold Soft.
- Icons: outline, 1.8 px stroke, rounded caps, 24 px grid (Lucide style).

## Signature motif

The dashed marigold "see more" fold line. Use it once per layout: under a hook, between a teaser and the full story, or as a divider.

## Voice and tone

Direct · Warm · Honest · Global. Short sentences, plain words. Sell outcomes (consistency, reach, leads), never automation.

| Moment | Say | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Post every week, in your own voice. | Unlock explosive viral growth with AI-powered synergy. |
| Safety | You approve every word. Nothing is automated on your account. | Our smart bots engage for you 24/7. |
| Button | Schedule post | Submit |
| Error | LinkedIn rejected the image. Replace it and try again. | Oops! Something went wrong |
| Empty state | No drafts yet. Paste a few old posts and we'll learn your voice. | Nothing to see here! |
| Pricing | $29 a month. Cancel any time. | Starting at just $29!!! |

No fake numbers, no "guaranteed viral", no hype, no emoji in product UI. Public copy is global: no rupee or India-only wording.

## Imagery

Real work: drafts, calendars, comment threads, people mid-task in natural light. No stock handshakes, no glowing robot brains, no fake screenshots of LinkedIn's own interface.
