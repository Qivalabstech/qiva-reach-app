# A3 · Goal and niche setup

**Status:** Approved by Razam Sir 13 Oct 2026. Built; PostHog event and help note still to add before "Accepted".

## User story
As a new user, I tell Qiva Reach my main goal and my niche, so ideas and posts fit what I'm trying to do. I can change both later.

## Acceptance criteria (locked)
User picks 1 of 4 goals and a niche (list or free text); both editable in settings.

## Screens
1. **/onboarding (step 1 of 2)** "What do you want from LinkedIn?"
   - Four cards, pick one: Grow my audience · Get leads and clients · Build authority in my field · Find a job
   - Then "What do you write about?": niche chips (SaaS founder, Consultant or coach, Marketing, Recruiting and HR, Finance, Tech and engineering, Sales, Design) **or** free-text field "Something else"
   - Button "Continue" (Marigold; disabled until goal and niche are set). Step 2 is A4.
2. **/app/settings** has "Goal and niche" with the same controls and "Save changes".

## Data
`profiles.goal` (one of `grow_audience`, `get_leads`, `build_authority`, `find_a_job`) and `profiles.niche` (text, max 80 chars). Writes through RLS as the signed-in user.

## Behaviour rules
- Free text is trimmed; empty after trim is rejected with "Add a niche so your ideas fit you."
- Changing goal or niche never deletes drafts.
- PostHog event: `goal_niche_saved`.

## Acceptance tests (Playwright + Vitest)
1. Cannot continue without both fields.
2. Chip niche and free-text niche both save and show again after reload.
3. Settings page shows saved values and updates them.
4. A user cannot read or change another user's goal or niche (RLS check).
5. Works at 390 px with keyboard only.
